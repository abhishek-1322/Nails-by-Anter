Our story

From a single desk to a studio you’ll love.

Nails by A began in 2018 at a small desk by a sunny window, with one artist, a handful of polishes and a long waiting list of friends. What they kept coming back for wasn’t just the designs — it was the care.

Today our Ludhiana studio is a calm, light-filled space where every appointment feels unhurried. We still hand-paint every detail, still sterilise every tool, and still take the time to understand exactly what you want.

Whether it’s a clean everyday manicure or bridal nails for the biggest day of your life, our promise is the same: your nails, your style — done beautifully.

Meet the artist

Hi, I’m Aanya — the “A” in Nails by A.

I fell in love with nail art while studying fashion design. Nails became my favourite canvas: tiny, personal and endlessly creative. After training in structured gel, Russian manicure and hand-painted art, I opened the studio to create the experience I always wanted as a client.

I personally train every artist on our team, and I still design most of our bridal sets myself.
