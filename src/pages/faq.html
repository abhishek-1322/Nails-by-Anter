Nail care & products

How long do nails last?

Gel polish typically lasts 2–3 weeks without chipping. Extensions and builder gel last 3–4 weeks before an infill is recommended. Regular polish from a classic manicure lasts around 5–7 days. Wearing gloves for cleaning and applying cuticle oil daily will help your set last longer.

What products do you use?

We use professional, salon-only gel systems that are vegan, cruelty-free and HEMA-free wherever possible — gentler on sensitive skin and lower in odour. Our cuticle oils and hand creams are nourishing, fragrance-light formulas. Ask your artist and they’ll happily show you the exact brands.

Will gel or extensions damage my natural nails?

Not when they’re applied and removed correctly. We prep with minimal filing and always remove product with a gentle soak-off — never by peeling or prying. Please don’t pick at your gel at home; book a removal instead.

How should I care for my nails afterwards?

Apply cuticle oil once or twice a day, wear gloves for cleaning and washing up, and avoid using your nails as tools. If anything lifts or breaks within 5 days, let us know and we’ll fix it free of charge.

Bridal & events

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes! Our Bridal Luxe package includes a design consultation, a trial session, bridal extensions with custom art and a luxury spa pedicure, plus a touch-up kit. We coordinate your design with your outfit, jewellery and mehndi. See bridal pricing.

When should I book my bridal nails?

We recommend booking your consultation 4–8 weeks before the wedding, a trial 2–3 weeks before, and your final set 1–3 days before the big day. Wedding season fills quickly, so earlier is better.

Can you do nails for the whole bridal party?

Absolutely. We host group bookings of up to 6 guests in the studio, and on-location services can be arranged for weddings. Get in touch for a tailored quote.

Appointments

How do I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with clean, product-free nails if you can (if you have existing gel, add a removal when booking). Bring any inspiration photos, avoid heavy hand cream on the day and plan to arrive 5 minutes early so you can settle in and choose your colours.

Can I bring my own design inspiration?

Please do! Screenshots from Instagram or Pinterest are perfect. Your artist will adapt the design to suit your nail shape and length, and let you know if it needs extra time.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We work mainly by appointment so we never rush a set, but we’ll always try to fit you in. Call or WhatsApp us to check same-day availability.

Policies

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows may be asked for a deposit on their next booking. Bridal bookings require a non-refundable deposit to secure your date.

What happens if I’m running late?

Please let us know as soon as possible. We hold appointments for 15 minutes; after that we may need to shorten your service or reschedule so the next guest isn’t kept waiting.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, all major debit and credit cards, and cash. Payment is taken at the end of your appointment.
